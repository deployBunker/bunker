1. Use the Bunker name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement, sponsorship, or affiliation with the Bunker project without explicit written permission.

3. Register domain names containing "bunker" that could mislead users about the source of the site.
4. Use the Bunker logo as your own product's logo or favicon.
